The Great War Archives

This work is a dual tribute by the Class of 1976: a memorial plaque at St George's, Ypres, and a digital archive of John Lyon School alumni killed in the Great War 1914–1918.

Serving from Gallipoli to the Somme, Africa to Mesopotamia — their names, their stories, remembered.
